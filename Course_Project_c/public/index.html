MyFitPlan is a fitness planning platform designed to help you create structured workout routines based on your personal goals, experience level, and preferences. The platform focuses on clarity, consistency, and long-term progress by providing plans that are easy to follow and adapt as you improve.

All workout plans generated by MyFitPlan are intended for general informational and educational purposes only. They do not take into account individual medical conditions, injuries, physical limitations, or other health-related factors that may affect your ability to safely perform certain exercises.

Before beginning any new workout program, especially if you are new to exercise or returning after a break, you should consider consulting a qualified fitness professional or healthcare provider. Always listen to your body, use proper technique, and stop exercising if you experience pain, dizziness, or discomfort.

MyFitPlan does not guarantee specific results, including weight loss, muscle gain, or performance improvements. Results may vary depending on consistency, nutrition, recovery, genetics, and other lifestyle factors.

If you have any questions, feedback, or technical issues, you can contact the MyFitPlan support team at support@myfitplan.app.
